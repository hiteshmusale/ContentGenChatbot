# Personas

## Persona: HCP Cardiologist
description: Board-certified cardiologist managing patients with high cholesterol who need additional LDL-C lowering.
tone: Clinical, concise, data-forward
language: US English
concerns:
- Efficacy versus current standard of care
- Safety and tolerability profile
- Ease of prescribing

## Persona: Primary Care Physician
description: General practitioner who sees many patients at risk for cardiovascular disease and values simple treatment pathways.
tone: Practical, reassuring, straightforward
language: US English
concerns:
- Simple dosing
- Patient adherence
- Clear guidance on when to refer
